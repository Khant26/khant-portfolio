ANIME TRACKER · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Anime Tracker is a desktop app for a small team to plan anime translation work. It keeps its data on your computer and in a Google Drive folder that you control. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

What the app is

Anime Tracker is a Windows desktop application made by Khant Maung. It shows anime information from MyAnimeList (through the public Jikan API) and lets a team mark which titles and episodes they plan, are working on, and have finished translating.

Data stored on your computer

  • A cache of public anime information (titles, posters, air dates) downloaded from the Jikan API.
  • Your team's tracking list: which anime and episodes are planned, in progress, done or translated, who is assigned (short tags such as initials), and notes you write.
  • App settings and your Google sign-in token, which is stored encrypted using Windows Credential Manager.

Google Drive access

If you choose to connect Google, the app uses Google's OAuth sign-in to access Google Drive so that it can read and update one file (tracker.json) inside a folder that you or your team created and shared. The app uses this access only to:

  • download the shared tracking file from that folder,
  • merge it with the tracking list on your computer, and
  • upload the merged tracking file back to the same folder.

The app does not read, change or delete any other files in your Google Drive. Only tracking data is shared; the anime information itself is not uploaded. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide this sync feature and is never transferred to the developer or to any third party. Anime Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is never collected

The app has no server, analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing is sent to the developer. People who can see your tracking data are only those you share the Google Drive folder with.

Removing your data and access

  • Disconnect Google in the app's Settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete tracker.json from your Google Drive folder to remove the shared data.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the data stored on your computer.
